# Demo video script (target 2:30, never over 3:00)

Record with Win+G (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS. Good light on your face. Close other apps. Turn off notifications.

| Time      | Show                                                                                                                        | Say                                                                                                                   |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00-0:10 | Game running, both waveforms moving                                                                                         | "This bomb listens to my heartbeat. That is my real pulse and breathing from a normal webcam."                        |
| 0:10-0:30 | Consent screen, tick the box, click Turn on camera                                                                          | "Nothing starts without consent. It reads pulse and breathing, saves nothing, and it's a game, not a medical device." |
| 0:30-0:50 | Calibration bar filling                                                                                                     | "It learns my resting pulse first, so it's fair to me."                                                               |
| 0:50-1:50 | Defuse: sit still, a wire cuts. Then tense up or breathe fast so the wires shake and the clock speeds up. Settle down again | "Calm cuts wires. Panic makes the clock run faster and the wires shake. I have to settle myself to win."              |
| 1:50-2:15 | Stillpoint: follow the circle, show pace match                                                                              | "Stillpoint teaches paced breathing, six breaths a minute."                                                           |
| 2:15-2:30 | Quick shot of electron/main.cjs, then the README privacy section                                                            | "The SDK runs in Electron's main process. No video or readings are stored. The data handling is in the README."       |

Rules:

- Show the waveform and the status line under it in the first 10 seconds.
- Do NOT say "actual heartbeat" unless the status line says "locked to detected beats".
- Do NOT say stress level, health, or diagnosis. Say calm, tension, game.
- Don't use the simulated signal anywhere in the video.
- Record a few takes. Use the one where the wires visibly shake.
